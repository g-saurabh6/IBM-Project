# Cybersecurity Policy and Incident Response Plan

## Small Business Cybersecurity Framework

### Project Overview

This project presents a cybersecurity framework designed for a small business. It focuses on protecting business information, responding to cybersecurity incidents, maintaining business operations during disruptions, and recovering IT systems after a disaster.

The project combines cybersecurity policy, incident response, business continuity, disaster recovery, and risk assessment into one practical framework.

## Objectives

- Protect business data and IT resources.
- Identify and manage cybersecurity risks.
- Provide a clear incident response procedure.
- Maintain essential business operations during disruptions.
- Restore systems and data after a disaster.
- Improve overall cybersecurity awareness.

## Project Components

### 1. Cybersecurity Policy

Defines security rules for:

- Passwords
- Access control
- Email and phishing
- Device security
- Data protection
- Backups
- Employee awareness

### 2. Incident Response Plan

Provides a structured process for handling security incidents:

1. Identification
2. Containment
3. Eradication
4. Recovery
5. Lessons Learned

### 3. Business Continuity Plan

Explains how critical business operations can continue during:

- Cyberattacks
- Power failures
- Internet outages
- Hardware failures
- Natural disasters

### 4. Disaster Recovery Plan

Defines procedures for:

- Data backup
- System restoration
- Recovery priorities
- Recovery Time Objective (RTO)
- Recovery Point Objective (RPO)
- Post-recovery monitoring

### 5. Risk Assessment

Identifies common cybersecurity risks and provides appropriate mitigation strategies.

Major risks considered include:

- Phishing
- Ransomware
- Malware
- Unauthorized access
- Data leakage
- Hardware failure
