# Risk Assessment

## 1. Introduction

Risk assessment is the process of identifying cybersecurity threats, evaluating their possible impact, and deciding how they should be controlled.

This assessment is designed for a small business environment.

## 2. Risk Assessment Matrix

| Threat | Likelihood | Impact | Risk Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phishing Attack | High | High | Critical |
| Ransomware | Medium | High | High |
| Unauthorized Access | Medium | High | High |
| Malware Infection | Medium | High | High |
| Data Leakage | Medium | High | High |
| Hardware Failure | Medium | Medium | Medium |
| Power Failure | Medium | Medium | Medium |
| Internet Outage | Medium | Medium | Medium |
| Lost/Stolen Device | Low | Medium | Low |

## 3. Phishing Attack

### Risk

Employees may receive fake emails containing malicious links or attachments.

### Impact

A successful phishing attack may result in stolen passwords, financial loss, or unauthorized access.

### Mitigation

- Employee awareness training
- Email filtering
- Multi-factor authentication
- Strong password policy
- Immediate reporting of suspicious emails

## 4. Ransomware

### Risk

Malicious software may encrypt important business files and demand payment.

### Impact

Business operations may stop and important data may become unavailable.

### Mitigation

- Regular backups
- Offline or protected backups
- Antivirus and endpoint security
- Security updates
- Employee awareness training

## 5. Unauthorized Access

### Risk

An unauthorized person may gain access to business systems or accounts.

### Impact

Sensitive information may be stolen, modified, or deleted.

### Mitigation

- Strong passwords
- Multi-factor authentication
- Role-based access control
- Regular account reviews
- Immediate removal of inactive accounts

## 6. Malware Infection

### Risk

Malicious software may enter the organization's systems through downloads, email attachments, or compromised websites.

### Impact

Malware can damage systems, steal information, or disrupt business operations.

### Mitigation

- Updated antivirus software
- Regular security updates
- Safe browsing practices
- Employee awareness
- Restricted software installation

## 7. Data Leakage

### Risk

Confidential business or customer information may be accidentally or intentionally exposed.

### Impact

Data leakage may cause financial loss, reputational damage, and loss of customer trust.

### Mitigation

- Access control
- Data encryption
- Secure file sharing
- Employee training
- Regular security reviews

## 8. Hardware Failure

### Risk

A computer, server, or storage device may fail unexpectedly.

### Impact

Important data and business services may become unavailable.

### Mitigation

- Regular backups
- Preventive maintenance
- Replacement hardware
- Disaster recovery procedures

## 9. Risk Treatment

The organization should prioritize risks according to their likelihood and impact.

Critical and high-level risks should receive immediate attention, while medium and low risks should be monitored and managed through appropriate security controls.

## 10. Risk Review

The risk assessment should be reviewed regularly and whenever there is:

- A major cybersecurity incident
- A change in business operations
- A new technology or system
- A significant change in the threat environment
