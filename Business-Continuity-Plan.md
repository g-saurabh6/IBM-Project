# Business Continuity Plan

## 1. Introduction

The Business Continuity Plan (BCP) explains how the organization will continue its important business operations during and after a disruption.

The plan is designed to reduce downtime, protect critical resources, and maintain essential services.

## 2. Objectives

The main objectives are:

- Continue critical business operations during disruptions.
- Minimize financial and operational losses.
- Protect employees, customers, and business information.
- Reduce downtime.
- Restore important services quickly.
- Provide clear responsibilities during emergencies.

## 3. Possible Business Disruptions

The business may experience disruptions caused by:

- Cybersecurity attacks
- Ransomware
- Power failure
- Internet outage
- Hardware failure
- Natural disasters
- Fire or physical damage
- Loss of important employees
- Third-party service failure

## 4. Critical Business Operations

The following operations should receive the highest priority:

1. Customer communication
2. Access to important business data
3. Internal communication
4. Financial operations
5. Customer support
6. IT and network services

## 5. Business Continuity Strategy

During a disruption, the organization should:

- Identify the cause and impact of the disruption.
- Protect employees and physical resources.
- Activate alternative communication methods.
- Use backup systems where available.
- Prioritize critical business services.
- Keep customers and employees informed.
- Restore normal operations as soon as possible.

## 6. Backup Communication

If normal communication systems are unavailable, the organization may use alternative communication methods such as:

- Mobile phones
- Alternate email accounts
- Messaging applications
- Emergency contact lists

## 7. Remote Work

If the office cannot be used, employees may continue essential operations remotely.

Remote workers should:

- Use secure internet connections.
- Use company-approved devices where possible.
- Enable multi-factor authentication.
- Avoid storing confidential information on personal devices.
- Follow the organization's cybersecurity policy.

## 8. Backup and Data Availability

Critical business data should be backed up regularly.

Backups should be:

- Performed on a regular schedule.
- Stored securely.
- Protected from unauthorized access.
- Tested periodically.
- Available for recovery when required.

## 9. Roles and Responsibilities

### Business Manager

- Activate the Business Continuity Plan.
- Coordinate important business decisions.
- Communicate with stakeholders.

### IT/Security Administrator

- Maintain IT systems and backups.
- Respond to technical problems.
- Restore affected systems.

### Employees

- Follow emergency procedures.
- Report incidents quickly.
- Protect company information.
- Support continuity activities.

## 10. Plan Testing and Review

The Business Continuity Plan should be tested periodically through exercises or simulations.

The plan should also be reviewed and updated whenever there are major changes to business operations, employees, technology, or risks.
