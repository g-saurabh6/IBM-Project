# Project Report

## Cybersecurity Policy and Incident Response Plan for a Small Business

## 1. Introduction

Cybersecurity is important for every organization, including small businesses. Small businesses may face threats such as phishing, malware, ransomware, unauthorized access, data leakage, and hardware failures.

This project provides a practical cybersecurity framework for a small business. It combines security policies, incident response, business continuity, disaster recovery, and risk assessment into a single framework.

## 2. Problem Statement

Small businesses often have limited resources and may not have a dedicated cybersecurity team. Because of this, security incidents can cause serious data loss, financial damage, and interruption of business operations.

There is a need for a simple and practical cybersecurity plan that helps a small business prevent threats, respond to incidents, continue essential operations, and recover affected systems.

## 3. Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- To develop a basic cybersecurity policy for a small business.
- To identify common cybersecurity risks.
- To create a structured incident response procedure.
- To develop a Business Continuity Plan.
- To develop a Disaster Recovery Plan.
- To define backup and recovery strategies.
- To improve employee cybersecurity awareness.
- To reduce the impact of cybersecurity incidents.

## 4. Scope of the Project

The project covers the following areas:

- Password and access control
- Email and phishing security
- Device security
- Data protection
- Backup management
- Incident response
- Business continuity
- Disaster recovery
- Risk assessment
- Employee security awareness

The framework can be adapted according to the size, requirements, and resources of a small business.

## 5. Methodology

The project follows a structured cybersecurity planning approach.

### Step 1: Identify Assets

Important business systems, data, devices, and services are identified.

### Step 2: Identify Threats

Common threats such as phishing, ransomware, malware, unauthorized access, and data leakage are considered.

### Step 3: Assess Risks

Each threat is evaluated based on its likelihood and potential impact.

### Step 4: Apply Security Controls

Security policies and preventive measures are defined to reduce identified risks.

### Step 5: Prepare Incident Response

A step-by-step process is created for identifying, containing, removing, and recovering from security incidents.

### Step 6: Prepare Business Continuity

Procedures are defined to keep critical business operations running during disruptions.

### Step 7: Prepare Disaster Recovery

Backup and recovery procedures are defined to restore affected systems and data.

## 6. Risk Management

The project identifies several major risks:

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Risk Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phishing | High | High | Critical |
| Ransomware | Medium | High | High |
| Malware | Medium | High | High |
| Unauthorized Access | Medium | High | High |
| Data Leakage | Medium | High | High |
| Hardware Failure | Medium | Medium | Medium |
| Internet Outage | Medium | Medium | Medium |

High and critical risks should receive priority and should be reviewed regularly.

## 7. Incident Response

The Incident Response Plan follows five major phases:

1. Identification
2. Containment
3. Eradication
4. Recovery
5. Lessons Learned

This approach helps the organization respond to security incidents in an organized manner and reduce their impact.

## 8. Business Continuity

The Business Continuity Plan ensures that critical business operations can continue during disruptions.

Possible continuity measures include:

- Remote work
- Alternative communication
- Regular backups
- Prioritization of critical services
- Emergency contact procedures
- Alternative IT resources

## 9. Disaster Recovery

The Disaster Recovery Plan focuses on restoring IT systems and business data after a major disruption.

Important recovery activities include:

- Identifying affected systems
- Assessing damage
- Isolating affected systems
- Restoring clean backups
- Verifying recovered systems
- Resuming normal operations
- Monitoring systems after recovery

## 10. Backup Strategy

Regular backups are an important part of the project.

The organization should:

- Perform regular backups.
- Keep secure copies of important data.
- Restrict access to backups.
- Test backups periodically.
- Maintain an off-site or cloud backup where possible.

## 11. Expected Benefits

Implementation of this framework can provide the following benefits:

- Better protection of business information.
- Faster response to cybersecurity incidents.
- Reduced business downtime.
- Improved data recovery.
- Better employee security awareness.
- Reduced cybersecurity risks.
- Improved preparedness for disasters.

## 12. Limitations

This project provides a general framework for a small business and does not represent a complete enterprise-level security system.

The actual implementation may require additional security technologies, trained security personnel, monitoring systems, and organization-specific policies.

## 13. Future Enhancements

The project can be improved in the future by adding:

- Security monitoring tools
- Automated incident detection
- SIEM integration
- Automated backup monitoring
- Vulnerability scanning
- Employee security training platform
- Security incident dashboard

## 14. Conclusion

This project provides a practical cybersecurity framework for a small business.

By combining cybersecurity policies, risk assessment, incident response, business continuity, and disaster recovery, an organization can become better prepared to prevent, handle, and recover from cybersecurity incidents.

The framework provides a foundation that can be further expanded as the business grows and its cybersecurity requirements increase.
